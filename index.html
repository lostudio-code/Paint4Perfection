Process

Perfection is in the preparation

Every exceptional paint job starts long before paint touches the wall. Here is how a project runs from first call to final walkthrough.

01
Consult

We walk the space with you, inspect surfaces and existing conditions, and understand what you want the room or the exterior to feel like when it is done. You get a written scope and a schedule — not a number scribbled on a card.

02
Prepare

Floors and furnishings are covered. Surfaces are washed, scraped, stripped, sanded, filled and caulked. Everything that should not receive paint is masked. Primer goes on where the substrate calls for it. This stage takes the longest and decides the finish.

03
Paint

The right product for the right application, applied with the right technique — brush, roller or spray depending on the surface. Coats are built up properly and given time to cure.

04
Perfect

Every edge, corner and transition is inspected. Touch-ups are completed, the site is cleaned, and we walk the finished project with you until you are satisfied with the last two percent.
About

We obsess over the details so you don't have to

Paint 4 Perfection is a full-service painting company serving residential and commercial clients across Westchester County, New York.

Founded and run by Frank D. from Hawthorne, the company has spent more than a decade on remodels, new construction, historic properties and high-tech offices. The work ranges from a single living room to a whole-house exterior, and the standard does not change with the size of the job.

What has not changed since the beginning is where the effort goes: into preparation. Everything visible in the finish was decided before the first coat.
